    At a glance

    What it does, in six lines

    Agentic tool calling, native to SalesforceMulti-step, multi-tool reasoning runs in Apex. It needs no MCP server, no middleware and no Agentforce licence.
    Several tools in one stepWhen a question needs unrelated lookups, the model asks for them together and the loop runs them in the same step.
    The model is a setting18 models from OpenAI and Anthropic today. An administrator switches between them from a dropdown.
    The user's own permissionsEvery query and update runs as the person asking. Updates stay off until an administrator turns them on.
    Answers with their evidenceEach answer lists the records behind it, with links and a CSV export of the full result.
    Deploys without configurationWorks on standard objects out of the box. An administrator adds custom objects, fields and business terms without code.

    How the numbers were made: Agentforce is 20 Flex Credits per action at $500 per 100,000 credits (list prices as of 2026). Holocron uses token counts logged from 107 tested questions (two-tool-call questions averaged 19,088 input and 493 output tokens) priced on GPT-6 Luna at $0.10 per million input tokens, $0.01 cached and $0.50 output. This is usage cost only. Licences are left out on both sides, and so is the effort to build and maintain a custom assistant. Full calculation.
